📖 How to use this book

  1. Follow in order. Each chapter builds on the last. Don't skip — the sequence is the point.
  2. Understand, don't memorize. Before running any command, say what you think it does. If you can't, you haven't learned it yet.
  3. Type, don't paste. Type commands yourself. Mistakes are how it sticks.
  4. Do every lab. 70% hands-on, 30% reading. A chapter isn't done until the lab is done.
  5. Tick the box on each chapter when you can explain it AND do its lab. Your progress saves automatically.

📌 An honest note about this book

  • This v1 is the complete map — every part, chapter, topic, tool and lab laid out so you always know where you are and what's next. The link never breaks.
  • The full deep text of each chapter is written in order, one at a time, and added here as you reach it. You're never waiting on the whole book — you get depth exactly when you need it.
  • Topics reflect what's used today (cloud, containers, APIs) and what's emerging (AI/LLM security) — not just old material.

⚖️ The one rule that matters

  • Everything here is for authorized testing only — your own systems, lab machines, CTF platforms, or targets with written permission.
  • Hacking a system you don't own or aren't authorized to test is a crime (in India: IT Act, 2000). No exceptions, no "just testing".
  • The skill and the ethics are one thing. A hacker who ignores this isn't advanced — just a criminal who got caught.
